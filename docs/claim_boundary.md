# Claim Boundary

The paper proves that every finite tableau entry is constructible and that the
diagonal converges to \(\pi\).

It does not claim:

- that \(\pi\) is constructible;
- that the constructibility boundary is crossed;
- that Richardson-Romberg extrapolation is new;
- that the method competes with modular-form algorithms in arithmetic cost;
- that the symbol \(\pi_g\) denotes a different numerical constant.
